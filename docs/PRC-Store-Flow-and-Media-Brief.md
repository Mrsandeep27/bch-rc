**Source:** Sir's handwritten notes + verbal walkthrough (reviewed ~300 stores). Captured 2026-07-01.

## The psychology spine (Sir's framework)
Every section serves one of four jobs, in order:
1. **Desire** — hero, categories, highest-drifted, why-RC, who-should-buy
2. **Trust** — "Are we good humans?" (one purchased → one donated)
3. **Reduce friction** — "Toy vs PRC" (lifetime warranty, genuine quality)
4. **Reduce perceived risk** — handled on the **product page** (details pending from Sir)

## CATEGORY PAGE — flow (when a category box is clicked)
Repeats the same rhythm, scoped to that category:
1. **Category hero banner** — that category's cars (photo)
2. **Moving slide text** — why buy *this* category
3. **Product collection** — the products in that category
