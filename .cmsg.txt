perf(video): cut hero + UGC video bandwidth (root cause of 43GB Jun-21 spike)

Root cause of the Vercel Fast-Data-Transfer spike: the homepage hero video
autoplays with preload="auto" on every visit (drift.mp4 6.3MB desktop /
3.2MB mobile), and UGC grid videos (4-9MB each) autoplay on scroll. A
traffic/ad spike on Jun 21 x a 6MB autoplay video = ~43GB.

- Hero: re-encoded keeping good resolution (900px desktop / 720px mobile,
  CRF 24/27, audio stripped, faststart). preload="auto" -> "metadata" so a
  visitor who bounces in 2-3s no longer pulls the whole clip.
- UGC: all 8 served clips re-encoded -53% to -61% (29MB -> 15MB total).
- Deleted local-only junk (public/fresh 300MB, public/ugc/_orig 38MB) +
  gitignored them. These were UNTRACKED so never deployed — not a bandwidth
  cause, just disk + repo hygiene.

NOTE: the drift hero is high-motion so it still can't go below ~5MB without
visible quality loss. The real structural fixes (pick one): trim the 11s loop
to ~6s (half size, same quality), serve mobile the poster image only (no
video — biggest win since traffic is mobile), or move the hero video to a
cheap video CDN (Bunny/Cloudflare Stream). Left for a decision.
